how it works

Three moves. Settled the moment it's due.

  1. 01 — Issue the GP issues a call against the LP roster on Canton.
  2. 02 — Settle the LP pays into an escrow and the GP's share is minted — atomically, one tx.
  3. 03 — Audit the auditor sees every obligation and every share, immutably.
ledger-enforced privacy

Each LP sees only their own slice.

Disclosure is enforced by Canton's privacy model, not a policy checkbox. LP 1 sees LP 1's obligation. LP 2 sees LP 2's. The auditor sees the full book. Nothing else leaks.
